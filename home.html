Financial inclusion at national scale

Most smallholder farmers in Indonesia lack formal credit histories. Traditional underwriting depends on branch visits, collateral paperwork, and slow manual review — leaving millions outside the banking system.

Agri-Access is an alternative credit scoring layer built for banks, rural lenders, and international development partners who need fast, explainable, and regulation-ready agricultural risk assessment.

Who we serve

  • Commercial and rural banks in Indonesia
  • Microfinance and cooperative lenders
  • Multilateral and development finance institutions
  • Agri-fintech platforms integrating via API

How it works

From farm coordinates to a bank-ready credit decision in about one minute.

1

Locate the farm

Select an Indonesian demo farm or enter latitude, longitude, crop type, and loan details.

2

Analyze satellite & weather

Pull NASA satellite tiles and weather signals to evaluate vegetation health and climate risk.

3

Score with AI ensemble

Random Forest + XGBoost models output credit score, SLIK rating, and Basel III risk parameters.

4

Explain & decide

SHAP drivers and Gemini-powered farmer explanations support transparent lending decisions.

Platform capabilities

Satellite risk intelligence

Multi-source imagery (Landsat, MODIS, VIIRS) with vegetation and land-use signals for any farm location.

Basel III compliance

Probability of Default, Loss Given Default, Exposure at Default, and Expected Credit Loss for regulatory reporting.

Explainable AI (SHAP)

Waterfall charts show which satellite, weather, and farm factors drive each credit outcome.

Indonesian SLIK alignment

Credit scores mapped to the 1–5 collectibility scale used in Indonesian banking supervision.

Farmer-friendly narratives

Gemini-generated explanations in Indonesian and English for branch staff and farmer communication.

API-first architecture

REST endpoints for integration into core banking, LOS, and partner pilot workflows.

Pilot case studies

Illustrative scenarios showing how feasibility is validated in real lending workflows.

Case 1: Rice working-capital loan screening

Region: West Java

Challenge: Small farmers with limited formal history require fast pre-screening.

Outcome: Screening cycle reduced from multi-day manual review to approximately one minute per farm profile.

Case 2: Palm oil portfolio risk triage

Region: Sumatra

Challenge: Bank team needs consistent risk bands across distributed branches.

Outcome: Basel III-style PD/LGD/EAD outputs enabled consistent internal risk prioritization across applications.

Case 3: Coffee cooperative lending support

Region: Central Java

Challenge: Loan officers need explainable decisions for cooperative discussions.

Outcome: SHAP-based drivers and bilingual summaries improved transparency during farmer-facing credit conversations.

Built for Indonesia's agricultural economy
